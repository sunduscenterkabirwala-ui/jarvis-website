The problems are money problems

Dead stock locks up cash. Credit written in a notebook quietly disappears. Unconfirmed parcels come back as losses. Jarvis was built to stop the leaks — and to bring sales in.

POS Billing + Offline Sales

Barcode billing that keeps working when the internet dies, with automatic stock deduction and a daily sales report.

Smart Inventory

Every purchase, sale, return, adjustment and transfer recorded — “where did the stock go?” always has an answer.

Real Profit + Cash Flow

Sales against cost, gross profit, expenses, credit and cash position. Know how much money actually stayed.

Attention Center + AI Assistant

Low stock, falling sales, unusual losses and overdue payments are flagged — with an actionable solution attached. The AI answers from real business data.

Khata + Recovery Alerts

Customer credit, payment history and due dates — plus recovery reminders that help bring trapped money back.

Dead Stock Detection

What is not selling, how much cash is trapped in it, and how to clear it without destroying the margin.

Online Store + WhatsApp Ordering

Your own website and catalogue with wholesale and retail prices, customer confirmation and verification before dispatch. Built for online sales, not decoration.

AI Marketing + Content

Captions, posts and reels from real product photos — published only after the owner approves. Less marketing time, better reach.

Alerts + Failure Monitoring

If an order fails, a sync stops or a backup breaks, the owner is notified immediately — with the way to recover.

Secure Multi-Shop Foundation

Every shop's data fully separate, staff permissions, audit logs and reliable backups. Safe to hand to the next shop.
